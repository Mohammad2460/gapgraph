Words you'll hear in the demo

Plain-English answers for teammates, users and judges.

Prerequisite graph
A map of a chapter where each idea is a dot (a "concept") and each arrow means "learn this first". An arrow from Derivatives to Chain Rule says you need derivatives before the chain rule. GapGraph builds this map from your upload.
DAG
Short for directed acyclic graph: arrows have a direction and never loop back. That matters because a loop like "A needs B, B needs A" would make it impossible to decide what to learn first. With a DAG there is always a valid study order.
BKT (Bayesian Knowledge Tracing)
A method that keeps a running estimate of how likely you are to know each concept. Every answer nudges the estimate up or down, allowing for lucky guesses and silly mistakes. GapGraph uses it to decide which concepts are weak.
Root-cause gap
The earliest weak prerequisite that explains a later failure. If you get Backpropagation wrong but the real trouble is Chain Rule, then Chain Rule is the root gap. Fixing it first is faster than re-studying the topic you failed.
Careless slip
A wrong answer on something you probably know, spotted by being very fast and very confident. GapGraph shows slips in orange and real gaps in red, so you don't waste time re-learning what you already understand.
